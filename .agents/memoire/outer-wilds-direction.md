User rejected the "one island = one mechanic" approach (Phare = tide+mirrors, Marais = water levels): "je veux plein d'énigmes différentes… comme Outer Wilds… une île n'est pas que sur la gravité… c'est juste tu fais des îles avec chacune leur énigme mais c'est pas ce que je recherche".
Decisions (2026-11-15):
- Central question driving the whole game: **« Où sont les Semeurs ? »** (like "what happened to the Nomai").
- Answer: **all Semeurs died from the brume, except the Gardienne, who became an inhabitant of the brume.**
- Hidden world: like Outer Wilds, nothing is ever *given* (no glasses/item at the end). From the very start, a clever or curious player could see the hidden people (N / darkness); you only understand it by progressing. Current N-from-start design fits.
- Island 2: keep validated parts but **enrich** with other puzzle types and investigation pieces.

- Full v4 decisions (7 islands, 6 named Semeurs, grande nuit cut by Iris, skeletons hidden early, single investigation with no bascule/2nd half, same ending for all) live in the repo's ENQUETE.md — read it before designing anything.

**Why:** they want a game that fills its length with varied puzzles and a real investigation, not themed islands.
**How to apply:** design islands as places holding pieces of several investigation threads, with mixed puzzle types; ask the user many questions with proposals (they explicitly asked to be questioned when unclear). See [[game-vision]], [[design-docs-first]].
