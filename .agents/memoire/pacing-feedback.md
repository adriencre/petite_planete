2026-11-14: after phase C the user said the island "va beaucoup trop vite": waking was instant, you could light a lantern right away "comme si c'était normal", the light gesture and root growth looked "cheap", and the notebook was empty/pointless. Fixed by: lying-down wake-up with flickering eyes (~23 s), slow silent camera reveal on exit, empty antenna recharged at a sun-stone, glyph drawn in the air → filaments → lantern bloom (first light = long cinematic), serpentine growing roots, notebook = ink map + auto place sketches + old notes as hints.

**Why:** the game's soul is calm + meaningful; mechanics must feel earned and the first time of anything must be an event.
**How to apply:** for every new mechanic, give it (1) a reason/discovery before use, (2) a long beautiful first occurrence with camera framing, (3) a shorter but still pretty repeat; leave "empty" moments; explain without text (antenna crackle, firefly, sketches). See [[game-vision]].
